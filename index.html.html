How to Play

  • Each player has 4 tokens.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the home area.
  • Click a highlighted token to move it.
  • Landing on an opponent token sends it home, except on safe cells.
  • Roll a 6 to get another turn.
  • First player to bring all 4 tokens to the center wins.
